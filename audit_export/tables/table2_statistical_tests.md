> **Table 2 Methodological Notes on Statistical Inference, Serial Dependence, and W5 Exclusion:**
> - **Serial Dependence & Primary Inference via Contiguous Block Permutation:** Standard Mann-Whitney $U$ assumes i.i.d. observations ($n=30$). Contiguous block permutation (`block_permutation_mwu`, $B=5000$) with adaptive block length $b \in \{3, 5, 8, 12\}$ determined by $\max(|r_1|)$ serves as the **primary two-sample inference procedure**, preserving serial dependence without parametric distribution assumptions. Across 146 contrasts, **0** remain significant under Holm-Bonferroni adjusted block permutation.
> - **First-Order $r_1$-VIF Variance Adjustment as Sensitivity Analysis Only:** Effective sample size $n_{\text{eff}} = n(1-r_1)/(1+r_1)$ is reported solely as sensitivity analysis. Under AR(1) simulation with $n=30$, $n_{\text{eff}}$ does not achieve exact 5% Type I error control (retaining ~8.2% false positives at $r_1=+0.4$ and ~12.7% at $r_1=+0.7$, while shrinking to ~0% for $r_1 < 0$). Under $r_1$-VIF Holm-Bonferroni, **93** contrasts remain significant and **6** contrast(s) marked `(NS*)` lose nominal significance.
> - **Workload W5 Segregation:** `python_native` (LangChain Core) in W5 is excluded from all pairwise latency/performance contrasts in Table 2 due to schema validation failure and confounded prompt truncation (see Table 8 and `w5_token_and_schema_audit.csv`).
> - **Multiple Testing Control:** Family-wise error rate across all pairwise hypothesis tests is controlled via the Holm-Bonferroni step-down procedure applied separately to standard, $r_1$-VIF, and block-permutation $p$-values.
